EXPENSE POLICY SUMMARY
Ref: POL-FIN-007

Effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all staff of Marwa Logistics LLC.

1. Allowances

Item | Limit (AED)
Meal allowance, domestic travel, per day | 120
Meal allowance, international travel, per day | 250
Hotel, per night | 650
Mileage, per km | 0.80

2. Receipts and claims

A receipt is required for any expense above AED 75. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days.

3. Approval limits

Claim amount (AED) | Approver
Up to 5,000 | Line manager
Above 5,000 and up to 25,000 | Finance Director
Above 25,000 | Chief Financial Officer
